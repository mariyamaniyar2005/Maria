Sudhar (सुधार) means improvement. That is the whole idea.

Our name is our promise. For more than forty years, SESCO has worked from Nallasopara West to make education, care and community life a little better for the people around us.

We are a trust, not a company. Everything we do is meant to be seen by the families it is meant for, and by the people who support it.

After you give

More ways to give

  • Give a little every month

    Steady gifts let us plan classes, camps and events months ahead instead of week to week.

  • Sponsor a student

    Support one child's books, uniform and school needs for the year and hear how they are doing.

  • Give in kind

    Books, notebooks, uniforms, clothes, dry rations. Call first so we can tell you what is needed right now.

  • Give on a special day

    Mark a birthday, anniversary or a loved one's memory with a gift in their name. We will send a thank-you note.

  • Partner as a business or shop

    Local businesses can sponsor an event, a camp or a term of classes and be thanked openly for it.

  • Ask your friends to give

    Share this page with your family and friends. Most of our supporters come through someone they know.

Common questions

Is my donation eligible for tax exemption?

Please ask our office before you donate and we will tell you what certificate we can give.

Can I visit before I give?

Yes. Please call or message first so someone can meet you. Our address is in the Contact section below.

What if my UPI payment fails?

Do not pay again straight away. Check your bank app, and if the money has left your account, send us a WhatsApp message with a screenshot. We will match it and confirm.

Can I stop a monthly gift?

Any time. It is your bank instruction, so you can cancel it in your bank app. You can also tell us and we will stop reminding you.
